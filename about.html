About

I'm Abhishek Dwivedi, and I make The Pulse: an occasional newsletter for the things that reward a second look. I write from a growing collection of paper notes, half-finished photographs, and walks that took longer than expected.

This is intentionally a small corner of the web. No rushing, no infinite feed—just a place to set a few observations down and return to them later.

Thanks for stopping by.
— Abhishek
